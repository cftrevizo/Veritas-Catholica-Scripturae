# Sources, Attribution, and Third-Party Notice

Veritas Catholica Scripturae is a reconciliation and publication layer built from prior work. Edition 1 does **not** claim authorship of upstream cross-reference data.

## Principal upstream sources

### NEUU Bible Cross-References Dataset
Repository: https://github.com/neuu-org/bible-crossrefs-dataset
Role: normalized/reconciled general cross-reference backbone and methodology; supplied package also contained raw upstream data used to reconstruct the backbone.

### OpenBible.info Cross References
Website: https://www.openbible.info/labs/cross-references/
Role: large community-weighted cross-reference source used by downstream projects.
License/attribution: follow the current upstream terms; OpenBible-derived material has historically required attribution.

### SoulLiberty / Treasury of Scripture Knowledge-derived data
Upstream family: Treasury of Scripture Knowledge / SoulLiberty-derived machine-readable cross-reference data.
Role: independent historical cross-reference family in the general backbone.
Public-domain and downstream-license status should be checked against the exact upstream files before republishing them outside this reconciled package.

### The Catholic Bible
Project: https://pypi.org/project/the-catholic-bible/
Repository/project materials identify a 73-book Catholic address framework and Catholic/Deuterocanonical cross-reference supplementation.
Role: primary Catholic-specific reconciliation source in Edition 1.

### Original Douay-Rheims structured dataset
Repository: https://github.com/janvier-s/original-douay-rheims
Role: structured historical Catholic Bible, annotations, and cross-references.
License: CC0 1.0 according to the supplied project materials.

### BibleViz — Chris Harrison and Christoph Römhild
Project: https://www.chrisharrison.net/index.php/Visualizations/BibleViz
Role: historical inspiration for Bible cross-reference visualization. VCS visualizations are independently generated from the Edition 1 reconciled dataset.

## Licensing rule
Third-party components remain subject to their upstream licenses, attribution requirements, and public-domain status. The VCS CC BY 4.0 notice applies to original VCS compilation choices, documentation, organization, metadata, and other material for which the project has licensing authority. It does not erase or replace upstream rights.
